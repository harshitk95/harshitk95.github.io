Balboa Moves

A working summary of the moves that make up Balboa - from the basic to Bal-Swing.

Dance · reference

Balboa is a swing dance born on the crowded floors of 1930s Southern California, danced to fast tempos in a close embrace. It splits into two flavours: Pure Balboa, where partners stay chest-to-chest and everything happens in the feet, and Bal-Swing, which opens the embrace up for turns, swivels and bigger shapes.
